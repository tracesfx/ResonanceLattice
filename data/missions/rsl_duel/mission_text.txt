Location: Over the Antiphon Institute
Date: 206.11.30

There have only ever been two Virtuosos. The first is the Lacrimosa, finished at last. The second was grown by Tri-Tachyon's Special Projects around a cracked core picked out of the Hush's graveyard: the Dies Irae, its seams the violet of the dead First Chorus.

Its pilot is Commander Isolde Raike, the best Special Projects ever paid for, and she has asked for exactly one thing: a duel. One ship each. No seconds.

***

One-on-one against a level 15 commander with every combat skill elite and her own Second Voice (+10% damage, +25% system recharge, +10% speed), in a Virtuoso with three S-mods. She has Bravura, Glissando and Stolen Time too. Starve her of readiness and keep your own above 92%.
