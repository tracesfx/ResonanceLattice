Location: A Cantorate outpost on the fringe
Date: 206.05.02

The Hegemony's AI Inspectorate has decided that the Cantor Array guarding this outpost is an unlicensed artificial intelligence. The Cantorate's answer - that a lattice core has a pitch, not a will - was entered into the record and ignored.

The Inspectorate cannot spare a fleet of its own, so it has hired one: a strike force of Hegemony surplus under a mercenary flagship with a past, an Unsung Virtuoso its captain dragged out of a Domain dock and never told anyone about.

Six petals turn on the Array's ring. They share one phase. When one falls silent, the others sing louder.

***

Destroy the Cantor Array. Every petal you kill pushes the others into Overtone (Phase-Locked Petals), and the Grand Chorus fires a lance from every petal at once: choose your kill order. An AI-flown Lattice squadron screens the station.
