Location: Vell, in the shadow of Tacet Station
Date: 206.03.11

No Cantorate hull is built. Each is grown: a lattice core seeded at Glasshouse, fed a year of pulsar light, and only then wrapped in steel. Before a new hull joins the choir it must sing under fire.

For today's proving the Luthiers have hired a Hegemony-pattern battle line from a mercenary company that asks no questions, and fielded the whole book against it: Grace Notes and Staccatos on the flanks, Refrains and a Tremolo in the line, a Cadence, a Fermata and a Conductor behind them.

At the head of the choir flies a ship that is not in the book at all. Its seams burn white.

Every Lattice hull, and the finished Virtuoso. Watch the Resonance bar: shields that take fire and guns that land fill it, and at 60 the next ship system use is an Overtone. The Virtuoso starts at 100% combat readiness, in Bravura; every Glissando costs it 1%, and Bravura ends below 92%.
